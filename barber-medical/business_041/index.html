About

About the shop

Shorty's has been in business for 33 years in downtown Coldwater. Step inside and you will feel like you have stepped into the 1900s, from the classic checkered floors to the antique barber chairs and sink. The art of traditional barbering is the point here: every customer gets great attention to detail, and the barbers still use straight razors for the neckline. Call or stop in today.
