A Tifton shop built around the chair

Knapps Barber Shop is a sole proprietorship in Tifton, Georgia, run by barber Corvin Jones. The shop serves men, women, and kids, and its listed services run from everyday haircuts and fades to hot towel shaves and facial treatments.

Walk in during opening hours, or call ahead — the shop is open Monday to Friday and Saturday mornings.
