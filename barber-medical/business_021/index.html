About the shop

A High Desert chair for the whole family

XCutz Barbershop describes itself as a destination for men, women and children's grooming, with a team of skilled barbers and stylists in a professional yet relaxed environment.

  • Walk-ins welcome, and appointments can be made — call ahead to arrange a time.
  • English and Spanish spoken in the shop.
  • Men, women and children all welcome.
  • Payment methods listed publicly: cash, Visa and Zelle.
