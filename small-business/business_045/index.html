Our Story

A studio that feels like family

North Main Dance Studio sits in a storefront on North Main Street, one block from the Weatherford courthouse. It is the only women's-only fitness facility in Weatherford — a place where women train together and children learn to dance, with trainers who adjust every session to the person in front of them.

From tiny dancers to older generations, the studio's classes are built around encouragement, not intimidation.
