About the shop

Pop Vintage Antiques is a Saugerties vintage shop on Partition Street. Its listing describes antiques and vintage items sold in a fun, upbeat atmosphere, including clothing, art, jewelry and pieces from the 1950s to the 1980s as well as older items.
