About Harris Brothers Electric

Harris Brothers Electric was founded by licensed electrician Brandon Harris alongside brothers Coltan and Brett Harris. Its public listing describes a family-centred approach built on trust, communication, professionalism and dependable service.

Service area: Dunlap, Sequatchie County, Pikeville, Whitwell, Jasper, Signal Mountain, Chattanooga, Dayton and surrounding Sequatchie Valley communities.
