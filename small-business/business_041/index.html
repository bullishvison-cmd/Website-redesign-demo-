Our Story

A local gym, opened in January 2025

Longevity Strength & Conditioning opened its doors in Dracut in January 2025, with a ribbon cutting on February 1. Co-owners Erin Gannon and Josh Valdez built it as a gym boutique — a smaller, more personal alternative to a big-box gym, where coaches know every member by name.

Erin is a yoga and core instructor, and the team brings a well-rounded approach to strength, conditioning, and everyday health.
