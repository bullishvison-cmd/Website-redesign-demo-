Our Story

Family owned, community grown

Wood's Family Fitness has been family owned and operated in Live Oak since 2018. Owner Rhonda Wood and the team built a gym where neighbours of every age and fitness level can train — from first-timers to seasoned lifters, and from busy parents to seniors staying active.

It is the only Silver Sneakers / United Healthcare provider listed in Live Oak, and group classes are part of membership, not an add-on.
