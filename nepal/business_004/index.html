Our Story

A luxurious hilltop destination above Bharatpur

Kabilas Resort and Funpark was founded in 2075 B.S. at Kabilas, in Bharatpur, Chitwan — a peaceful escape offering both comfort and excitement, overlooking the hills and the Narayani River valley.

  • Hilltop setting with views over Narayanghat city and the Narayani River
  • Stays, restaurant and bar, swimming and funpark attractions on one site
  • Free WiFi and free parking for guests
  • Room service for guests staying at the resort
  • A family destination — relaxation and play in one visit

Why Choose Kabilas

Stay, eat, swim and play — without leaving the hill

Most resorts ask you to choose between rest and activity. Kabilas puts both on the same hilltop.

One destination, whole day covered

Stays, dining, swimming and the funpark are all on site, so families do not need to plan transport between activities.

Views you cannot get in the city

The hilltop position looks over green hills, Narayanghat and the Narayani River — the setting is part of the stay.

Easy to reach, easy to park

Near Jugedi on the Narayanghat–Muglin Highway route, with free parking for guests arriving by car.

For families and groups

The funpark and pools give children plenty to do while adults enjoy the restaurant, the bar and the quiet hilltop air.
