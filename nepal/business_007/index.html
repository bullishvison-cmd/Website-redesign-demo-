About

Beside one of Kathmandu's most visited landmarks

Shamatha Spa and Salon is located opposite the world-renowned heritage site of Boudhanath Stupa, in Boudha, Kathmandu. Visitors can pair a walk around the stupa with a massage, facial or manicure, or a hair appointment — all under one roof.

The salon describes its approach as world-class quality service using advanced, innovative technology and modern equipment, delivered by highly skilled and experienced beauticians, hair specialists and massage therapists.
