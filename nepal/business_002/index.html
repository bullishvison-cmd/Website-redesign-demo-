A calm, central stay — a short walk from Thamel.

Hotel Lapha in Lainchaur, Kathmandu, offers comfortable rooms, an on-site restaurant and bar, a terrace, free WiFi and free private parking, with a 24-hour front desk and airport transfers.

Rooms & services

Room types and services as listed for the hotel on booking directories. No room rates are shown here — please contact the hotel for current availability.

Rooms for every party

Single, twin, queen, king, triple, quadruple and family rooms — each with a seating area, flat-screen TV, desk and private bathroom with shower.

Restaurant & bar

An on-site restaurant and bar, with room service, breakfast in the room, packed lunches, and buffet, à la carte and continental breakfast options.

Terrace & practical comforts

A terrace for fresh air, free WiFi throughout the property, free private parking, luggage storage, and a 24-hour front desk.

Close to Thamel, away from its noise

Hotel Lapha sits on Pakhalati Marg in Lainchaur — a short walk from Thamel, and within easy reach of Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhu and the city's main sights. Guests note the convenient location, helpful staff and comfortable rooms, and the hotel's own restaurant is a regular mention in recent stays.
