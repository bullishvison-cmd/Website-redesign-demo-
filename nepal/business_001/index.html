Fine Dining Room

Our Story

A terrace garden above Thamel

Since March 2016, Spice Garden has worked together with Krua Thai Nepal, adding authentic Thai and Continental cuisine alongside its Indian menu — with the same management and team of cooks. Guests can choose fine dining seating or the open terrace garden restaurant, with a small bar at the corner and live music.
