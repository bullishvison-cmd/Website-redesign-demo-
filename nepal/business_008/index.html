About

A Thamel retreat inside Chhaya Center

Sunrise Spa sits on the 3rd floor of Chhaya Center, one of Thamel's major shopping centres — easy to reach for visitors staying in the area and for Kathmandu residents alike.

The spa describes itself as a sanctuary where stress melts away, combining massage, hammam, sauna and beauty services with beauty and wellness training. Gift vouchers are also available.
